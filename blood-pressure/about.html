About The Vitality Report

Independent wellness journalism for readers 45 and over

The Vitality Report covers healthy aging in plain English: circulation, sleep, nutrition, energy, and the everyday habits that research keeps pointing to. We believe wellness information should be readable, honest, and respectful of the reader's intelligence.

What We Cover

  • Healthy Aging Series: deep-dive guides on the health markers that matter most after 45
  • Research, translated: what new studies actually say, without the hype
  • Reader stories: real experiences shared with our editorial team, always clearly labeled and never presented as typical

Our Editorial Standards

  • We write for humans, not algorithms
  • We cite the general body of research and mainstream health institutions
  • We clearly label advertising content and reader stories
  • We remind readers, in every health article, that nothing we publish replaces their own doctor's guidance

How We're Funded

The Vitality Report is free to readers and supported by advertising. When readers click through to partner presentations and make purchases, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to the reader. This never determines which topics we cover or how we cover them.
